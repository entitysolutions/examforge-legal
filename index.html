Common questions

  • Are these real exam questions? No. They are original practice questions, and none of them comes from a real exam.
  • Which exams does it cover? The exam picker in the app lists every exam you can study.
  • Are you connected with the companies behind these exams? No. ExamForge isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any certification body or exam vendor.
  • Will this get me a pass? We can't promise that. Your practice score is a percent of the questions in the app. It isn't on the real exam's scale and doesn't predict a result. Where a mock shows pass or fail, that's the app's own estimate from its practice questions, not the exam vendor's scoring.
  • Is the content up to date with the exam? Exams change, and the questions may contain errors. The app shows which version of the official objectives the questions follow, with a link to them. Check those before you rely on anything here.
  • Do I need an account or an internet connection? There is no account. Any exam on your iPhone works with no connection. You need one only to download another exam, to get updated exam files, and to open a link to a website.
  • Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone. It doesn't sync to iCloud or your other devices and we can't see it, so we can't restore it if it's lost.
  • How do I erase my progress? Open Settings, go to Your Data and tap Erase All Progress. This can't be undone.
  • How do I turn reminders on or off? Open Settings and use Daily Reminders under Notifications. Reminders are off until you turn them on.
  • Does it cost anything? Nothing is for sale in the app today. If that changes, purchases would go through Apple.
