Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

ExamForge is a practice app for IT certification exams, provided by Entity Solutions LLC. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your progress is saved on your iPhone and the app doesn't send it to us.

What is saved on your iPhone

  • Your answers. For each question you answer: the exam, the question, the answer you chose, whether it was right, and when.
  • Your study sessions. The exam, the study mode, which questions were in the session, when it started and ended, and the score.
  • A mock exam in progress. Its questions, your answers and flags, your place and the time left, until you finish or discard it.
  • Your bookmarks. The questions you saved and when you saved them.
  • Your study plan. If you answer the questions the app asks when it first opens: when your exam is, and its date if you give one, where you're starting from, what worries you most and how much time you have each day. They set your daily goal and the plan on the Study screen, and you can change them in Settings, under Your plan.
  • Your profile. The name you type, if you type one, which is used only to greet you on the Study screen. Also your daily goal, your streak, the exam you picked and your reminder setting.
  • Exam files. Any exams the app has downloaded. These hold questions and nothing about you, and they are left out of backups because they can be downloaded again.

None of this is synced to iCloud or to your other devices, and we can't see it. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the backup may include it. That copy is held by Apple or on your computer, and we can't read it.

What leaves your iPhone

The app contacts our server to check for new or corrected exam files and to download them. It checks when you open the app, at most once a day, and when you pull down in the exam picker. It downloads the exam you pick and updates to the exam you are studying. It doesn't do this in the background.

Like any website, the server sees your IP address and the request itself, such as which file was asked for and when. The company hosting the server can see those same details. To make sure a request comes from the ExamForge app, your iPhone uses Apple's App Attest, which creates a key that stays on your iPhone; our server keeps only its public half, the time it was added and a counter that stops old requests being reused. That key isn't linked to you or your progress. To limit abuse, the server keeps a daily count of requests for each key and for a scrambled form of each IP address, and deletes those counts after a day. Your answers, sessions, bookmarks and profile are never sent. There is no account, cookie, advertising identifier or tracking code involved.

Some exams are built into the app and any exam on your iPhone works with no connection, so you can practice without one. Two things you choose to do can also reach someone else:

  • Opening a link. "Official exam objectives", "Read more" and the web copies of this policy and the terms open in your browser. The site you reach sees what any website sees, such as your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies. Our own pages have no tracking code, but the company hosting them can see those same details.
  • Emailing us. If you write to support, we receive your email address and whatever you send, and we use it to answer you.

Permissions and reminders

The only permission ExamForge asks for is notifications. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Daily reminders are off until you turn them on, in Settings or when the app first opens. When you do, iOS asks for your permission and the reminder is set up on your iPhone for the time you pick. It doesn't come from a server of ours. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings, under Notifications.

Apple

  • The App Store. Downloading the app from the App Store is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Purchases. Nothing is for sale in ExamForge today. If that changes, Apple would handle the payment and tell the app whether a purchase is active. We wouldn't see your payment details.
  • Crash reports and statistics. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and summary statistics. ExamForge has no code of its own for this. You can turn it off in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, open Settings, go to Your Data and tap Erase All Progress. That removes your answers, sessions, saved mock exams, bookmarks, streak and profile from this iPhone and cancels your reminder. It can't be undone. Deleting the app removes them too. A backup you already made keeps its copy until that backup is replaced or deleted. Downloaded exam files hold nothing about you, and they stay until you delete the app.

We hold no copy of your progress, so there is nothing for us to delete and no account to close. The app key record described above holds no name, email or progress, and it stays on our server after you delete the app. To have a support email deleted, write to us at support@entitysolutions.co.

Children

ExamForge isn't aimed at children. The app sends us no progress and nothing that identifies anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on our website.
